Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

The short version

Bolt's Busy Builders does not collect personal information from children or anyone else. This website has no sign-ups, no forms, no advertising and no tracking or analytics cookies.

This website

This site is a simple information page about the show. We do not ask for, collect, store or sell any personal information. We do not use cookies or analytics. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts for its typeface; those providers may process standard technical data (such as IP address) to deliver the page, under their own privacy policies.

Our videos on YouTube

Our episodes are published on YouTube and are designated "Made for Kids", in line with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). On Made for Kids videos YouTube switches off personalised advertising, comments and other data-collecting features. Viewing on YouTube is governed by Google's Privacy Policy; we do not receive any personal information about individual viewers.

Our production tools (BBB Pipeline)

We use a private production tool, "BBB Pipeline", which connects through Google's official APIs to our own YouTube channel only. It uses the access we grant to:

  • upload and manage our own videos, thumbnails, captions and playlists (YouTube Data API), and
  • read aggregated, anonymous performance statistics for our own channel, such as view counts and watch time (YouTube Analytics API).

It does not access any other person's account or data. Access tokens are stored securely and used only for the purposes above. BBB Pipeline's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can review or revoke third-party access to a Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Our content is made for children aged 2–5 and is intended to be watched with or chosen by a parent or caregiver. We never knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. Questions? Email boltsbusybuilders@gmail.com.
